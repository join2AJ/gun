## Full description (≤ 4000 chars)
Feel the difference between a Glock and a .50 cal.

CALIBRE is a realistic gun sound simulator and field manual with more than 70 legendary firearms from 1891 to today — from the Lee–Enfield and M1 Garand to the AK-203, INSAS, Tavor, HK416 and Barrett.

■ EVERY GUN SOUNDS, SHAKES AND FLASHES DIFFERENTLY
• Live-synthesised gunshots: supersonic crack, muzzle blast, echo and each weapon's own mechanism
• Vibration built from the actual sound — strength-controlled on supported phones
• Flashlight flashes with every shot, with a pattern unique to each muzzle device
• Spent cases bounce and ring on concrete, thud in sand and snow

■ REALISTIC HANDLING
• Single shot, burst, full-auto, bolt-action and pump-action
• Animated reloads — magazines drop to the ground, new ones slide in
• Manual or automatic reload, barrel heat, recoil and camera shake
• Shake your phone to fire

■ 7 ENVIRONMENTS
Firing range, desert, Himalayan snow, jungle, city street, night ops and indoor range — each with its own echo, ground, weather and ambience.

■ FIELD MANUAL
• Effective range vs. maximum bullet travel
• Ammunition drawn to scale, bullet weight and muzzle energy
• Rate of fire, accuracy and heat behaviour
• Reliability in desert, snow, jungle, mud and high altitude
• Step-by-step "how it works" for every action
• History, conflicts and trivia

■ FILTER BY
Era (WWI, WWII, Cold War, Modern) · Type (pistol, SMG, rifle, sniper, LMG, machine gun, shotgun) · Origin (India, USA, Russia, China, Europe, Israel, Japan & Korea) · Used by (Army, Navy, Air Force, Special Forces, SPG, NSG, Para SF, MARCOS, Garud, Police)

■ FREE TO PLAY
25 weapons free forever. Unlock any premium weapon or environment for 24 hours by watching a short video, or upgrade once to Full Arsenal or Pro. No accounts and nothing to grind. Plays offline.

For entertainment and education only. Sounds are synthesised simulations; specifications are typical published values.
