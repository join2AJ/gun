Privacy Policy

Calibre — Gun Simulator & Field Manual · ARTIN Studios · Effective 2 October 2026 (updated for ads & purchases)

Summary

ARTIN Studios does not run any servers and does not itself collect personal data. There are no accounts. The Android app shows ads through Google AdMob and sells optional one-time upgrades through Google Play; those Google services process some data as described below. The website version has no ads and no purchases.

Advertising (Android app)

The free version shows a banner on the weapon list and optional rewarded videos you choose to watch. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad-interaction and diagnostic data, in order to serve, measure and protect ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), you are asked for consent first and can change your choice any time in Store → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Buying Remove ads or Pro turns banner ads off.

Purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details; the app only receives confirmation of which upgrades your Google account owns, so it can unlock them and restore them on a new device. See the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app uses on your device

Vibration — to simulate recoil. Flashlight — only if you switch on the flashlight effect, to flash with each shot (the Android app controls the LED directly and never captures camera images; the website version asks the browser for camera access only to reach the flashlight and does not record or transmit anything). Motion sensors — only if you switch on "Shake to fire". These are used live on your device and nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Data stored on your device

Your settings, favourites and preferred fire modes are kept in local storage on your device so the app remembers them. They never leave your device and are deleted if you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Internet

The game itself is bundled inside the Android app and plays offline. Internet access is used only to load ads and to talk to Google Play for purchases. The website version loads its own files from its host and nothing else.

Children

Calibre is not directed at children under 13. Ads are requested as general-audience, non-child-directed traffic.
