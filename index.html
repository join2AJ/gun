Ready the range

Arsenal uses sound, vibration and light to simulate gunfire.

  • Turn your volume up — headphones recommended.
  • Every weapon vibrates differently (Android browsers).
  • Your flashlight can flash with each shot (asks for camera permission, Chrome on Android).
  • Best played with the phone held sideways.
  • Contains rapid flashing lights — use “Reduce flashing” if you are photosensitive.
